How the setup controls work

Load Demo Policy

Loads your current saved policy, epoch, root, and list values into Overview and Rulebook Lab.

Create / Reset

Create starts your first saved policy. Reset to Blank Template clears the saved rule lists and creates a new epoch after confirmation.

Calculate / Save

In Rulebook Lab, Calculate Changes validates and previews the draft. Save New Epoch commits a materially changed policy.

Continue to Deploy

After saving, open Deploy. Your saved policy root, epoch, active wallet, and network are loaded automatically for registry deployment.

Policy field guide

Regulated assets are governed token covenant IDs. The allowlist permits recipients when enabled; the blacklist blocks recipients. Frozen holders and outpoints block ordinary transfers. Lawful-order actions remain unavailable until their deterministic schema is approved.

Forced Burn

        
Burn regulated Testnet-10 tokens under a verified Demo Lawful Order without the holder signature. A third-party target holder must be frozen in the currently published Demo Policy. The active issuer/controller wallet may burn regulated tokens held in its own wallet without freezing itself. The active Registry-authority wallet signs the control and fee inputs. Any unburned token remainder returns to the same target wallet, and the issuer controller supply is updated automatically after the forced-burn receipt is accepted.
