        About KCC20 actions
        

          

            
Controlled Token

            
A live-chain-verified KCC20 token controlled by the active wallet. Stored records are re-verified before use.

            
Issue Tokens

            
Creates additional supply within the token’s fixed maximum supply.

            
Burn Tokens

            
Permanently removes the selected raw amount and automatically updates supply accounting.

          

          

            
RAW Amount

            
Token amount in raw units. Human display uses RAW ÷ 10^decimals.

            
Pending Supply Recovery

            
Available only when a Burn was accepted but its automatic supply update failed. It never repeats the accepted Burn.
