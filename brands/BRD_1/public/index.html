What the subscription includes

  • Token Depot tenant-backed mainnet license access for Energy token issuance.
  • Energy dashboard access for production-token workflow and reward tracking.
  • Eligibility to redeem qualifying MWHK energy tokens with NC Solar Electric.
  • Support for getting started with the production-token process.

What the tokens are for

  • Energy tokens document verified production milestones.
  • NC Solar Electric accepts eligible tokens for published equipment discounts.
  • Tokens are not a promise of cash value, profit, or investment return.
  • Discount redemption requires active subscription status and program eligibility.
